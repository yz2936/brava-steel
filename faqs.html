Common questions about BRAVA's products, logistics, lead times, and customer service.

We have warehouse locations in China, Korea, Australia, and Indonesia, with customer service available across both US Eastern and Asia-Pacific time zones — making BRAVA easy to reach from anywhere in the world.

We use a variety of packing styles to suit your needs and ensure the integrity of your products throughout international transit. Our standard option is polywooden cases, with custom packaging available on request — including end caps, plastic sleeves, banded bundles, and weatherproof wrapping for sea freight.
