BRAVA — Industry Photos
=======================

Each industry section on /industries.html has a 1200 × 320 banner. Right now
they show a stylized SVG illustration. To replace those with real photographs,
just drop a JPG with the matching filename into THIS folder. The site will
automatically use the JPG (the SVG is loaded as a transparent fallback only
when the JPG is missing — no HTML changes required).

EXPECTED FILENAMES (drop your own JPG with the exact name)
----------------------------------------------------------
  oil-gas.jpg            → Oil & Gas section
  marine.jpg             → Marine section
  automotive.jpg         → Automotive section
  petrochemical.jpg      → Petrochemical section
  valve-instrument.jpg   → Valve & Instrumentation section
  heat-exchanger.jpg     → Heat Exchangers section

RECOMMENDED IMAGE SPECS
-----------------------
  • Aspect ratio: 30 : 8 (wide banner) — a 1500×400 or 1800×480 px JPG works well
  • Format: JPG, ~80% quality, < 400 KB ideal
  • The site automatically applies a navy gradient overlay, so any photo with
    decent contrast will look good — bright outdoor shots are best
  • Avoid photos with heavy embedded text or watermarks

PHOTO SUGGESTIONS BY INDUSTRY
-----------------------------
  Oil & Gas        — offshore platform, drilling rig, FPSO, subsea wellhead
  Marine           — cargo ship, ferry, naval vessel, port crane, ROV
  Automotive       — car exhaust manifold, fuel rail, factory line, EGR
  Petrochemical    — refinery skyline at dusk, cracker furnace, distillation tower
  Valve & Instrument — pressure gauge cluster, control panel, instrument tubing
  Heat Exchanger   — shell-and-tube HX cutaway, U-tube bundle, condenser

WHERE TO FIND ROYALTY-FREE PHOTOS
---------------------------------
  • Unsplash — https://unsplash.com  (search e.g. "oil rig", "cargo ship")
  • Pexels   — https://pexels.com
  • Pixabay  — https://pixabay.com
  • Adobe Stock or Shutterstock if you need licensed editorial-quality shots
  • Wikimedia Commons for historical / public-domain industrial photography

If you'd rather keep the SVG illustrations, just don't drop in JPGs — the
SVGs already match BRAVA's brand palette and are perfectly serviceable.
