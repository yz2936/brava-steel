BRAVA Stainless Steel at the Offshore Technology Conference 2026

Trade Show 📅 May 4–7, 2026 📍 NRG Park, Houston, TX 🕐 5 min read

BRAVA Stainless Steel is heading to Houston this May to join more than 30,000 energy professionals at the Offshore Technology Conference (OTC) 2026 — the world's foremost event for the development of offshore resources in fields including drilling, exploration, production, and environmental protection.

From May 4 through May 7, 2026, our team will be on the ground at NRG Park, ready to discuss the stainless and alloy tubing solutions that move the offshore industry forward — from instrument tubing for subsea control modules, to super-duplex S32750 / S32760 piping for the most aggressive seawater service.

What We're Showcasing

OTC 2026 brings together operators, EPC contractors, OEMs, and supply-chain partners from across the offshore energy industry. BRAVA's focus this year is on three product lines that have seen the strongest demand from offshore clients in 2025–2026:

Duplex & Super-Duplex Tubing

Duplex 2205 (S31803 / S32205) and super-duplex 2507 (S32750) and S32760 grades are increasingly the default specification for chloride-rich service. We'll be showcasing our full inventory range — including dual-certified material in stock at our Korean and Chinese hubs.

Sour-Service NACE MR0175 Material

Following our 2025 capability expansion, BRAVA now stocks duplex tubing certified to NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 for upstream operators in H₂S environments. Visit us at OTC to discuss certification documentation and lead times.

Instrument & Control Tubing

For subsea control modules, hydraulic power units, and topside instrumentation — our TP316/L and TP304/L instrument tubing meets ASTM A269 to tight dimensional tolerances, with full mill test certificates and PMI testing on request.

"OTC is where the offshore industry sets its priorities for the next 12 months. Our team will be there to listen, to learn, and to put the BRAVA supply chain to work for our customers." — BRAVA Sales Team
